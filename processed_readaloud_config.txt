ReadAloud Configuration for Multilingual EPUB
============================================================

Add these patterns to ReadAloud (Pattern/Replace mode):

Language: AR
  Pattern: ^\s*\[READ:AR\]\s*
  Replace: 
  Marker: [READ:AR]
